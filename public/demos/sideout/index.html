BEFORE YOU ARRIVE

GOOD TO
KNOW.

First visit or fiftieth, here are a few quick answers.

Do I need to bring a paddle?+

Bring your own if you have one. For this concept, loaner paddles are available at the front desk.

Can beginners join open play?+

Absolutely. Open play welcomes every level, and the first-timer session is an easy way to learn the basics.

What should I wear?+

Comfortable athletic clothes and court shoes with non-marking soles are a good fit.

Is this a real booking?+

No. SIDEOUT is a fictional club concept and this page is a frontend demo. No booking is submitted.
